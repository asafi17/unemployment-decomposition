Slide 2: People are saying...
Two main stories: 
1) Eligibility/composition story: A larger share of the unemployed may be made up of workers with weak UI eligibility, especially *new entrants* with no prior work history and possibly some *migrant groups*. 

2) Claim-propensity story. Even among plausibily eligible workers, people may be less likely to claim UI because the costs of claiming are high relative to the expected benefit. One candidate mechanism that some displaced white collar workers receive severance packages or expect re-employment and therefore do not bother filing. 

In this we ask whether variation in state-time composition of unemployment helps explain variation in UI claims intensity and IUR.
